How to play

The game

  • Break from behind the head string. The table is open after the break; the first ball you pocket decides your group: solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  • Pocket your whole group, then the 8‑ball. Tap a pocket to call it before shooting the 8.
  • You keep shooting while you pocket your own balls.
  • A foul (scratch, wrong ball first, no cushion after contact) gives your opponent ball in hand anywhere on the table.
  • On an open table you may not hit the 8 first.
  • After a scratch on the break the cue ball goes behind the head string, and it must cross the string before hitting a ball that is also behind it.
  • You lose the rack if you pocket the 8 before your group is cleared, in an uncalled pocket, together with your last group ball, or on a foul (for example the cue ball follows it in). Scratching on the 8 without pocketing it is just a foul.

Controls

  • Aim – drag anywhere on the table to rotate the cue; tap to aim at a spot.
  • Fine aim – roll the ribbed wheel for tiny adjustments.
  • Shoot – pull the cue down on the power bar and let go. Slide back to the top to cancel.
  • Spin – tap the cue-ball button, then drag the red tip mark. Double-tap to centre.
  • Ball in hand – drag the cue ball to place it.

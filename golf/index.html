How to play
AimDrag left or right on the upper screen. Tap Map and tap a spot to aim straight at it.
SwingOn the lower pad, drag down to take the club back: the arc shows the power, the gold notch is the power that reaches the pin, and full power clicks into place (the 100% mark lights up green). Then swipe up: a short flick is enough, you don't need to get back to where you started. A straight swipe flies straight; a curved one draws or fades. Pulling well past full is an overswing: more power, less control. To cancel, hold still at the bottom and let go.
Tap meterPrefer tapping? Choose Swing control: Tap meter in Settings. Tap the pad to start the power bar, then tap again when it reaches the power you want (it fills, hangs at full, then falls back). The shot flies straight.
TempoA brisk, even flick is right. Only a frantic snap is Rushed (the face wanders); creeping back up is Decel (a little less distance).
PuttDrag down to set the pace (the readout shows the flat-roll distance), then release. Changed your mind? Slide back up to the start, or off to the side, before you let go. Use Read to see the slope and −/+ to change the putt range.
ClubsThe caddie picks a club for every lie. Use ◀ ▶ to change it.
ShotsTap Shot beside the club for a special shot: a Draw or Cut that bends around trouble (aim away from the target and the curved line shows where it lands), a low Stinger or Punch under the wind, a high Flop that stops dead, or a Bump & run. It lasts one stroke.
CoursesPick a course on the main menu before a new round: parkland, desert, island or links. Each keeps its own best round.
RulesOut of bounds: stroke and distance. Water: drop (+1) or replay (+1). Pick up at double par.
